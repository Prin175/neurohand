Welcome to NeuroHand

Imagine moving your hand. When NeuroHand recognizes it, a virtual hand moves on screen.

  • This guide takes about 5 minutes.
  • Your plan grows from your own results.
  • Not a medical device. If you have a medical condition, ask a healthcare professional first.

Imagine, don’t move

Each trial asks you to imagine closing your left or right hand for a few seconds.

Do

  • Feel your fingers squeeze slowly
  • Breathe normally

Don’t

  • Move your real hand
  • Strain or hold your breath

Put on the headset

  1. Charge the headset.
  2. Place C3, Cz and C4 across the top of your head.
  3. Move hair so each one touches the scalp.
  4. Check the signal on the Device page.

Stay safe

  • Check in before each session.
  • Pause or stop anytime.
  • Rest between sets.
  • Never catch up on missed sessions.
  • Feeling unwell? Contact a healthcare professional.

Your data

NeuroHand saves your results to build your plan:

  • Recognition for each hand
  • Session length
  • Check-in and signal quality
