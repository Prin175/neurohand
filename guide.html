Welcome to NeuroHand

NeuroHand helps you practise motor imagery: imagining a hand movement without making it. An EEG headset reads your brain activity, and a virtual hand on screen moves when the movement you imagine is recognized.

  • About 5 minutes. This guide covers how training works, how to wear the headset, how to stay safe, and ends with a short practice round.
  • Your plan grows with you. After 3 short starting sessions, NeuroHand builds a personal plan from your own results.
  • A research prototype. NeuroHand is not a medical device. If you are recovering from a stroke or have a medical condition, check with a healthcare professional before you start.

What is motor imagery?

Imagining a movement uses many of the same brain areas as making it. Each trial asks you to imagine closing your left or right hand for a few seconds.

Do

  • Imagine it from inside your body, as if you feel your own fingers squeeze.
  • Picture a slow, steady squeeze, like holding a soft ball.
  • Breathe normally and keep your face and shoulders relaxed.

Don’t

  • Don’t actually move your hand. Keep it resting still.
  • Don’t watch yourself from the outside, like in a video.
  • Don’t strain or hold your breath to make it work.

Put on the headset

  1. Charge the headset. An orange light means it needs charging.
  2. Place the electrodes across the top of your head, from ear to ear. C3 sits over the left side, Cz in the middle, C4 over the right.
  3. Move hair aside so each electrode touches the scalp.
  4. Open Device to check the signal. Each electrode should show good before you start.

C3 picks up right-hand imagery and C4 picks up left-hand imagery, because each side of the brain controls the opposite hand.

Stay safe and comfortable

  • Check in before every session. If you feel a little tired, the session is shorter with longer rests. If you feel uncomfortable, there is no session that day.
  • Stop whenever you need to. Pause or stop at any time. Stop if you feel tired, dizzy, get a headache, or feel uncomfortable.
  • Rest between sets. Each session has rest breaks. Take longer if you need it.
  • There is no catching up. Missed sessions are never added to later days.
  • Get help if something is wrong. If discomfort continues or worries you, contact a healthcare professional.

Your data and your plan

To build your personal plan, NeuroHand saves after each session:

  • How often your left- and right-hand imagery was recognized
  • Session length and whether you stopped early
  • Your check-in answer and the headset signal quality

In this prototype, data stays in this browser. You can delete it at any time on the My plan page. In a research study, data would be handled as set out in the study’s ethics approval.
