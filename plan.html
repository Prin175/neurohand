    How the plan is used

    1. Each training day, you check in on the Home page.
    2. Feeling good: the session follows this plan.
    3. A little tired: one set fewer and longer rests.
    4. Feeling uncomfortable: no session. Rest, and contact your clinician if it continues.
    5. Missed sessions are never added to later days.
